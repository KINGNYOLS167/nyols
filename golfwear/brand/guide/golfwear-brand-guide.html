GOLFWEAR · BRAND GUIDE

A G drawn as a ring, with a flag on the pin flying out through its opening: the moment the ball drops. Set beside a calm, widely spaced wordmark. Elegant on a polo, clear on a sleeve.

Colours

Fairway green carries the brand; brass is only for the flag and small accents. Ask the printer to match a physical swatch: screens and fabrics shift colour.

On the product

Suggested placement. Send the files in the print folder to your printer or embroiderer: they are vector and already at these sizes.

Arm sleeve

Wordmark along the outer forearm, reading from the wrist up. 120 mm long, starting 60 mm above the cuff. One colour: green on white sleeves, white on black or grey.

Polo

Mark on the left chest (the wearer's left), 30 mm wide, about 180 mm below the shoulder seam. Embroider in green with a brass flag, or one thread colour.

Type

For banners, labels and the website, use Jost (free, Google Fonts), the typeface the wordmark is drawn from. Headings in Jost Medium with wide letter spacing, text in Jost Regular.
